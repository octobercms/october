# Contributing to October CMS

Thank you for your interest in improving October CMS. Contributions from the community are welcome and appreciated. This document explains how to contribute and the terms under which contributions are accepted.

Please also review our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). We expect all participants to act with courtesy and respect.

## Ownership of contributions

October CMS is licensed software distributed under an [End User License Agreement](./LICENSE.md). Everything in this repository, including any community-contributed code, is the property of Responsiv Pty Ltd.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that:

* Your contribution becomes part of October CMS and may be distributed under our license terms.
* You have the right to submit the work you are contributing.
* You are not knowingly contributing code owned by anyone else, or code that is subject to an incompatible license.

If you are contributing work created within the scope of your employment, or using your employer's resources, please confirm you have permission to contribute it before submitting. Do not submit code that belongs to your employer or to another project unless you are certain you have the right to do so.

This does not remove your authorship. You retain credit for your work in the project history.

## What not to change

To keep the project coherent and to protect its commercial model, please do not submit changes that:

* Alter anything related to licensing, purchasing, editions, or feature-targeting.
* Circumvent or disable license validation, update checks, or paid features.
* Maintain a long-term public fork of October CMS as an alternative distribution.

## Before you start

For anything beyond a small fix, please open an issue first to discuss the change. This avoids duplicated effort and lets us confirm the change fits the direction of the project before you invest time in it.

* Report bugs and propose features through the [issue tracker](https://github.com/octobercms/october/issues).
* For questions and general discussion, [join us on Discord](https://discord.gg/gEKgwSZ).

## Reporting security vulnerabilities

Do not report security vulnerabilities through public issues or pull requests. Please review [our security policy](https://github.com/octobercms/october/security/policy) for how to report them privately.

## Submitting a pull request

1. Fork the repository and create your branch from the appropriate base branch.
2. Keep each pull request focused on a single change. Unrelated changes should be submitted separately.
3. Ensure your contribution is your own work, or that you have the right to submit it under our license terms.
4. Add or update tests where appropriate, and make sure the existing test suite passes.
5. Write a clear description of what the change does and why.

## Coding standards

Please follow the same standards used throughout the project:

* [PSR-4 Autoloader](https://github.com/php-fig/fig-standards/blob/master/accepted/PSR-4-autoloader.md)
* [PSR-2 Coding Style Guide](https://github.com/php-fig/fig-standards/blob/master/accepted/PSR-2-coding-style-guide.md)
* [PSR-1 Basic Coding Standard](https://github.com/php-fig/fig-standards/blob/master/accepted/PSR-1-basic-coding-standard.md)

Match the conventions of the surrounding code. Check sibling files for structure, naming, and approach before introducing new patterns.
